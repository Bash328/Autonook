About AutoNook

We build the quiet, unglamorous fixes that save real hours.

AutoNook started with a simple observation: most small and mid-sized businesses lose hours every week to tasks that don't need a human doing them — not because owners don't know it, but because the automation options out there are built for enterprises, priced for enterprises, and explained in language nobody has time to decode.

So we set out to do it differently: plain explanations, fair pricing, and automations that actually fit how a local business runs day to day.

What we believe

The nook is a small, deliberate space

Everything we take on has to earn its place — that's the standard we hold ourselves to.

Plain over clever

If we can't explain what an automation does in one sentence, we haven't finished the job.

Fit before scale

We'd rather get one workflow right than bolt on features you'll never use.

Nothing left to guess

You'll always know what's running, what it's doing, and how to reach us if something breaks.

Local, wherever you are

Fair pricing, responsive support, and tools that work with what local businesses actually use — whichever market you're in.
