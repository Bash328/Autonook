Custom builds

If it eats an hour a week, it's worth automating.

Custom builds are for the parts of your business that don't fit a template — the exact workflow only you have, built to match how you actually work.

What you can ask for

If it's repetitive, it's fair game

You don't need to know the technical side — just describe what's slow, and we'll figure out how to fix it. Here's the kind of thing that usually qualifies.

Repetitive admin

Quoting, invoicing, data entry, follow-ups — the paperwork that eats a morning every week.

Customer-facing flows

Onboarding new clients, sending confirmations, chasing replies — without you typing the same message twice.

Internal alerts

Stock running low, a deadline coming up, something needing approval — flagged before it becomes a problem.

Reporting

Numbers pulled together automatically instead of copy-pasted by hand every month.

How it works

Four steps, no jargon

You don't need to speak "automation" for this to work — that's our job.

01

Tell us what's slow

A quick message about the task that's eating your time — that's all we need to start.

02

We map it out

We look at the tools you already use and work out the simplest way to fix it — no rip-and-replace.

03

We build & test it

You see it working before it touches anything live, so there are no surprises.

04

It goes live

And if you'd rather not think about it again, we can keep watching it for you.

Not sure this is the right fit?

If your task is common enough that other businesses deal with it too, a ready-made kit might get you there faster and cheaper. Otherwise, just tell us what's slow.
